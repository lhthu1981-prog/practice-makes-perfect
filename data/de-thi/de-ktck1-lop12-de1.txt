## PART A. LISTENING (1.5 points)

### Section 1 (Questions 1 – 3)
[HƯỚNG DẪN]
You will hear a talk about a Vietnamese scientist. Listen and decide whether the following statements are True (A) or False (B). You can listen TWICE.
[NGHE]
Speaker: Good morning, everyone. In today's history club meeting, I'd like to tell you about Luong Dinh Cua, one of Viet Nam's greatest agricultural scientists. He was born in 1920 in Soc Trang, in the Mekong Delta. As a young man, he went to Japan and studied at Kyushu University, where he received a scholarship thanks to his excellent results. After many years abroad, he could have stayed there and enjoyed a comfortable life. However, in 1952, he decided to return home with his family because he wanted to help Vietnamese farmers. At that time, most local rice took a long time to grow and gave low yields. Luong Dinh Cua worked day and night in the fields, and he created new rice varieties that grew faster and resisted pests. In total, he developed more than forty crop varieties. Personally, I find his story truly moving. He chose hard work in the fields over fame and comfort, and that is why farmers still remember him today.
[CÂU HỎI]
1. Luong Dinh Cua studied at a university in Japan.
A. True
B. False
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: Chi tiết nghe trực tiếp: "he went to Japan and studied at Kyushu University".

2. He returned to Viet Nam because he could not find a good job in Japan.
A. True
B. False
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: Ông về nước vì muốn giúp nông dân Việt Nam ("because he wanted to help Vietnamese farmers"), và lẽ ra có thể ở lại sống thoải mái.

3. The speaker admires Luong Dinh Cua for putting his country's needs before his own comfort.
A. True
B. False
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: Người nói thấy câu chuyện "truly moving" và nhấn mạnh ông chọn làm việc vất vả ngoài đồng thay vì danh tiếng, sự thoải mái → ngưỡng mộ.

### Section 2 (Questions 4 – 6)
[HƯỚNG DẪN]
You will hear a phone conversation between Minh, a Grade 12 student, and a receptionist at a coffee academy. Choose the best answer A, B, C or D. You can listen TWICE.
[NGHE]
Receptionist: Good afternoon, Green Bean Coffee Academy. How can I help you?
Minh: Hi. I saw your poster about the weekend barista course. Could you tell me when it starts?
Receptionist: Sure. The next course starts on Saturday, the fifth of December. Classes run from eight to eleven a.m. every Saturday and Sunday for four weeks.
Minh: That's perfect. I'm still at school, so I can only study at weekends. Do I need any experience?
Receptionist: Not at all. The course is for beginners. You'll learn how to make different kinds of coffee and how to serve customers politely.
Minh: Great. Actually, I'm hoping to find a part-time job at a café next summer, so I think this course will really help my CV.
Receptionist: I'm sure it will. Many of our students find jobs right after the course. The fee is one point two million dong, and you can register online.
Minh: Thank you so much! I'll talk to my parents tonight, and then I'll fill in the form on your website.
[CÂU HỎI]
4. When does the next barista course start?
A. On Sunday, 5th December
B. On Saturday, 5th December
C. On Saturday, 15th December
D. On Saturday, 6th December
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: "The next course starts on Saturday, the fifth of December."

5. Why is Minh interested in the course?
A. He plans to open his own coffee shop.
B. He wants to get a part-time job at a café.
C. His school requires a work experience course.
D. He has worked as a waiter before.
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: Minh nói "I'm hoping to find a part-time job at a café next summer" (diễn đạt lại mục đích).

6. What will Minh probably do next?
A. Discuss the course with his parents before registering.
B. Pay the fee at the academy right away.
C. Visit the café to meet the teachers.
D. Look for another course with lower fees.
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: Suy luận từ câu cuối: tối nay nói chuyện với bố mẹ rồi mới điền đơn đăng kí trên website.

## PART B. LANGUAGE IN USE (2.75 points)

Last month, our members **(8)** _______ an old market building when the owner came out and invited them inside for tea!
This year, you can:
- join us every Sunday morning. Each session lasts **(9)** _______ hour and a half.
- learn basic techniques. You don't need to worry **(10)** _______ your drawing skills – beginners are welcome.

= **Register on the club's fan page before 30th November!**
[CÂU HỎI]
7.
A. organised
B. has organised
C. is organising

8.
A. drew
B. have drawn

9.
A. a
B. the
C. Ø (no article)

10.

11.

B. beauty
C. beautify
D. beautifully
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: Sau mạo từ "the" và trước "of" cần danh từ: the beauty of old streets.

12.
A. which
B. that
C. who

## PART C. READING (2.5 points)

### Reading passage (Questions 18 – 27)
[HƯỚNG DẪN]
Read the following passage and choose the best answer to each of the questions from 18 to 27.
[BÀI ĐỌC]
Have you ever met someone who finds it difficult to answer the simple question "Where are you from?" For third culture kids, or TCKs, this question can be surprisingly complicated. TCKs are young people who spend a large part of their childhood in a country different from their parents' home country. Their parents may be diplomats, business people, teachers or aid workers who move abroad for work. As a result, these children grow up between two or more cultures. **[I]**
The term "third culture" describes the special mix that these children create. The first culture is the one of their parents, and the second is the culture of the country where ##they## are growing up. The third culture is a blend of both, shaped by shared experiences with other internationally mobile families. A boy born to Korean parents but raised in Viet Nam, for instance, might speak Korean at home, Vietnamese with friends and English at an international school. **[II]**
Growing up this way brings many advantages. TCKs are usually ##flexible## and can adapt to new places quickly. Many of them speak several languages and understand that people may see the same situation in very different ways. Research suggests that they often become open-minded adults who are comfortable working with people from different backgrounds. ~~The more countries they live in, the more easily they make friends with strangers.~~ **[III]**
However, this lifestyle also has its challenges. Because they move so often, TCKs may have to say goodbye to close friends again and again. Some feel that they do not fully belong anywhere. When they return to their parents' country, they may experience "reverse culture shock": they look like local people, but they do not share the same habits, jokes or childhood memories. This can make them feel ##isolated## for a while.
Experts believe that families and schools can help. Parents can keep traditions from home alive, such as cooking family recipes and celebrating festivals, while teachers can encourage TCKs to share their stories in class. **[IV]** With the right support, the experience of living between cultures can become a valuable gift rather than a burden.
[CÂU HỎI]
18. The word "they" in paragraph 2 refers to ___.
A. their parents
B. internationally mobile families
C. third culture kids
D. the first and second cultures
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: "the country where they are growing up" – chỉ những đứa trẻ (TCKs) đang lớn lên ở nước đó, không phải bố mẹ.

19. According to paragraph 1, why do TCKs grow up in a foreign country?
A. They are sent abroad to study at international schools.
B. Their families want to escape difficult lives at home.
C. Their parents move abroad for work.
D. They win scholarships to study abroad.
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: Đoạn 1: "Their parents may be diplomats, business people, teachers or aid workers who move abroad for work."

20. The word "flexible" in paragraph 3 is closest in meaning to ___.
A. stubborn
B. adaptable
C. careful
D. talented
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: flexible = linh hoạt, dễ thích nghi = adaptable (câu sau giải thích: "can adapt to new places quickly").

21. The word "isolated" in paragraph 4 is OPPOSITE in meaning to ___.
A. lonely
B. confused
C. tired
D. connected
Đáp án: D
Giải thích: isolated (bị cô lập, lạc lõng) >< connected (gắn kết); lonely là từ gần nghĩa.

22. Which of the following is NOT mentioned as an advantage of being a TCK?
A. getting better marks at school
B. speaking several languages
C. adapting to new places quickly
D. being comfortable working with people from different backgrounds
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: Đoạn 3 nêu A, B, C; bài không nhắc đến điểm số ở trường.

23. What is paragraph 4 mainly about?
A. The difficulties that TCKs may face
B. How TCKs make new friends
C. Why TCKs prefer to live abroad
D. How parents can support TCKs
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: Đoạn 4 mở đầu bằng "this lifestyle also has its challenges" và liệt kê các khó khăn.

24. Which of the following best paraphrases the underlined sentence in paragraph 3?
A. They make friends with strangers easily, so they decide to live in more countries.
B. Although they live in many countries, they rarely make friends with strangers.
C. Making friends with strangers is easier in some countries than in others.
D. Living in more countries makes it easier for them to make friends with strangers.
Đáp án: D
Giải thích: So sánh kép "The more … the more easily …" = càng sống ở nhiều nước càng dễ kết bạn với người lạ.

25. It can be inferred from paragraph 4 that reverse culture shock happens because ___.
A. TCKs' experiences differ from those of local young people
B. TCKs look very different from local people
C. TCKs cannot speak their parents' language
D. local people refuse to talk to TCKs
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: Các em trông giống người bản xứ nhưng không có chung thói quen, câu đùa, kí ức tuổi thơ → trải nghiệm khác biệt.

26. Where in the passage does the following sentence best fit?
"Such activities help them see that having more than one home is something to be proud of."
A. [I]
B. [II]
C. [III]
D. [IV]
Đáp án: D
Giải thích: "Such activities" chỉ các hoạt động vừa nêu ở đoạn 5 (nấu món ăn gia đình, đón lễ hội, kể chuyện trên lớp) → vị trí [IV].

27. Which of the following best summarises the passage?
A. Third culture kids usually become diplomats like their parents.
B. International schools are the best places for children of foreign workers.
C. Growing up between cultures brings both benefits and challenges, and the right support makes a difference.
D. Reverse culture shock is the biggest problem for young people today.
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: Bài nêu khái niệm, lợi ích (đoạn 3), khó khăn (đoạn 4) và cách hỗ trợ (đoạn 5).

## PART D. WRITING (1.25 points)

### Arrangement (Questions 28 – 32)
[HƯỚNG DẪN]
Choose the best arrangement of utterances or sentences to make a meaningful exchange or text in each of the following questions from 28 to 32.
[CÂU HỎI]
28. a. Lan: Thanks, Mai! I'm so happy that all my practice has paid off.
b. Mai: Guess what? I've just heard that your painting won first prize in the city art contest. Congratulations!
c. Mai: You really deserve it. Let's celebrate with some bubble tea after school.
A. a – b – c
B. c – a – b
C. b – c – a
D. b – a – c
Đáp án: D
Giải thích: Báo tin vui và chúc mừng (b) → cảm ơn, bày tỏ niềm vui (a) → khen và rủ ăn mừng (c).

29. a. Staff: I'm sorry to hear that. Could you tell me your order number, please?
b. Customer: Hello. I'm afraid there's a problem with the trainers I ordered from your website.
c. Staff: Thank you. I've checked it, and you're right. We'll send you the correct size tomorrow at no extra cost.
d. Customer: Sure, it's 2471. I ordered size 39, but you sent me size 42.
e. Staff: Good morning, Sporty Feet customer service. How can I help you?
A. e – b – a – d – c
B. b – e – a – d – c
C. e – a – b – d – c
D. e – b – d – a – c
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: Chào hỏi (e) → phàn nàn (b) → xin lỗi, hỏi mã đơn (a) → cung cấp mã và nêu lỗi (d) → giải quyết (c).

30. Dear Thao,
a. For example, his grandmother showed me how to make num ansom, a sticky rice cake wrapped in banana leaves.
b. Last week, my classmate Thach Minh invited me to celebrate Chol Chnam Thmay, the Khmer New Year, with his family.
c. I hope you can join us next year so that you can see these beautiful traditions too.
d. During the three-day festival, I learnt a lot about Khmer customs.
e. After that, we all went to the pagoda, where people built sand mountains and splashed water on each other for good luck.
Best wishes,
Hai
A. d – b – a – e – c
B. b – d – e – a – c
C. b – d – a – e – c
D. c – b – d – a – e
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: Lời mời (b) → ý chung: học được nhiều phong tục (d) → ví dụ 1 "For example" (a) → hoạt động tiếp "After that" (e) → lời chúc kết thư (c).

31. a. A year earlier, during an expedition in the Central Highlands, he had discovered the Langbian Plateau, where the city of Da Lat was later built.
b. In 1889, he joined the Pasteur Institute in Paris, and a few years later he travelled to Indochina.
c. He lived in Nha Trang for most of the rest of his life and died there in 1943.
d. Alexandre Yersin was born in Switzerland in 1863 and later studied medicine in Lausanne, Marburg and Paris.
e. After isolating the plague bacillus in Hong Kong in 1894, he became famous around the world.
A. d – b – a – e – c
B. b – d – e – a – c
C. d – e – b – a – c
D. d – b – e – a – c
Đáp án: D
Giải thích: Trình tự thời gian: 1863 (d) → 1889 (b) → 1894 (e) → "A year earlier" = 1893, phải đứng sau mốc 1894 (a) → cuối đời (c).

32. a. For instance, in many neighbourhoods, residents grow vegetables together and share the harvest with elderly neighbours.
b. Some people argue that such land should be used for car parks because cities lack parking space.
c. In my opinion, empty plots of land in cities should be turned into community gardens.
d. However, more car parks only encourage more cars, while gardens make the air cleaner and bring neighbours closer.
e. First of all, these gardens provide fresh food and green space for people who live in crowded areas.
A. c – e – a – b – d
B. c – a – e – b – d
C. c – e – a – d – b
D. e – c – a – b – d
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: Quan điểm (c) → luận điểm "First of all" (e) → ví dụ "For instance" (a) → ý kiến phản biện (b) → bác bỏ "However" (d).
