### Task 1. Pronunciation
[HƯỚNG DẪN]

## PART 3. LEXICO
### Multiple choice
[HƯỚNG DẪN]
Choose the correct answer A, B, C or D.
[CÂU HỎI]
16. This building looks ________ a big guitar.
A. as
B. like
C. different
D. from
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: look like + danh từ = trông giống như.

17. Hoa's painting is ________ Lan's. Hoa paints animals, but Lan paints flowers.
A. like
B. as
C. different from
D. the same
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: Hoa vẽ động vật, Lan vẽ hoa → hai bức tranh khác nhau: be different from.

18. The bamboo flute is not as popular ________ the guitar among students.
A. than
B. like
C. from
D. as
Đáp án: D
Giải thích: So sánh không bằng: not as + tính từ + as.

19. The school choir gave a wonderful ________ last night.
A. perform
B. performer
C. performance
D. performing
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: Sau tính từ "wonderful" cần danh từ; give a performance = biểu diễn.

20. The students ________ a lot of fun at the music festival.
A. did
B. had
C. made
D. took
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: Cụm cố định: have fun = vui chơi.

21. Could you ________ me a favour and turn the music down?
A. do
B. make
C. take
D. give
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: Cụm cố định: do someone a favour = giúp ai một việc.

22. The museum staff ________ the old paintings carefully so they don't get damaged.
A. look for
B. look at
C. look after
D. look up
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: look after = chăm sóc, bảo quản; look for = tìm; look at = nhìn; look up = tra cứu.

23. A person who goes to see a museum or a gallery is a ________.
A. composer
B. painter
C. visitor
D. actress
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: visitor = khách tham quan; composer = nhà soạn nhạc; painter = họa sĩ; actress = nữ diễn viên.

24. In "I prefer pop music to rock", the verb "prefer" means ________.
A. to like one thing more than another
B. to dislike something
C. to make something
D. to look at something
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: prefer A to B = thích A hơn B.

25. If an art class is optional, it is ________.
A. a class you must attend
B. a class you can choose
C. a very difficult class
D. a very boring class
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: optional = tự chọn (không bắt buộc).

## PART 5. WRITING
### Sentence Completion with Key Word
[HƯỚNG DẪN]
Complete the second sentence so that it has the same meaning as the first. Use the word given. Do NOT change the word given. You must use between TWO and FIVE words, including the word given. Type ONLY the missing words.
[CÂU HỎI]
36. Classical music is slower than rock music.
KEY WORD: **NOT**
→ Rock music ________ classical music.
Đáp án: is not as slow as|isn't as slow as|is not so slow as|isn't so slow as
Giải thích: Đổi so sánh hơn sang so sánh không bằng: Rock music is not as slow as classical music.

37. This painting looks similar to the one in the museum.
KEY WORD: **LIKE**
→ This painting ________ the one in the museum.
Đáp án: looks like|is like
Giải thích: look similar to = look like (trông giống).

38. Painting is not the same as drawing.
KEY WORD: **DIFFERENT**
→ Painting ________ drawing.
Đáp án: is different from|is different to
Giải thích: not the same as = be different from.

39. I like drawing more than painting.
KEY WORD: **PREFER**
→ I ________ painting.
Đáp án: prefer drawing to
Giải thích: like A more than B = prefer A to B.

40. My drawing and my dad's drawing are equally good.
KEY WORD: **AS**
→ My drawing ________ my dad's.
Đáp án: is as good as
Giải thích: equally good = so sánh bằng: as + tính từ + as.
