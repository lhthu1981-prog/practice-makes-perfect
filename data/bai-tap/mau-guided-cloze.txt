# DONG HO PAINTINGS

Dong Ho paintings are a {{26}} folk art of Viet Nam. They come from Dong Ho Village in Bac Ninh Province. The artists print pictures on special paper {{27}} wooden blocks.

People often buy them before Tet to {{28}} their houses. The colours are {{29}} from natural materials such as leaves and wood. Today, only a few families still make these paintings, so the village is trying to {{30}} this art for young people.
