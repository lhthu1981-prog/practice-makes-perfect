BÀI TẬP SẮP XẾP CÂU / LƯỢT LỜI – UNIT 4 (URBANISATION) – 10 CÂU
Cách nhập: Quản trị → Kho bài tập → + Thêm bài tập
  • Tên bài:   Unit 4 – Arrangement (10 questions)
  • Cấp độ / Lớp / Kỹ năng: chọn theo lớp (vd THPT – Lớp 12 – Viết)
  • Dạng bài:  Thông thường (nhiều dạng câu)
  • Thời gian: 15
  • Hướng dẫn làm bài: Choose the best arrangement (A, B, C or D) of utterances or sentences to make a cohesive and coherent exchange or text.
Sau đó bấm ⚡ Dán nhanh nhiều câu → dán toàn bộ phần bên dưới đường kẻ → Thêm vào bài → Lưu bài tập.
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1.
a. Minh: Not really. The cost of living is going up, so many young people can’t afford to buy a house here.
b. Lan: Hi, Minh! Are you happy with the changes in your neighbourhood?
c. Lan: I see. Urbanisation brings a lot of changes, but it can also cause problems.
A. a – c – b
B. c – a – b
C. b – a – c
D. c – b – a
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: b – a – c. b chào và hỏi; a trả lời “Not really…”; c phản hồi “I see…” để kết thúc.

2.
a. Mai: Oh dear! That’s common during rush hour. The more crowded the city gets, the worse the traffic jams become.
b. Mai: Yes, but the city has built a new metro line recently. Have you ever tried it?
c. Tom: Hi, Mai! Sorry I’m late. I got stuck in a traffic jam for almost an hour.
d. Tom: Not yet, but this is the first time I’ve heard about it. Let’s take it home together!
e. Tom: I know. Many people have moved here to seek better jobs, so there are more and more vehicles on the roads.
A. c – e – a – b – d
B. c – a – e – b – d
C. a – c – e – d – b
D. c – a – b – e – d
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: c – a – e – b – d. c xin lỗi vì đến trễ; a đáp “Oh dear!”; e “I know.” đồng tình và nêu lý do; b “Yes, but…” đưa giải pháp và hỏi; d “Not yet…” trả lời câu hỏi.

3.
a. However, urbanisation has also caused some problems, such as traffic jams and higher unemployment.
b. First, many rice fields have been replaced by high-rise buildings and shopping centres.
c. In my opinion, the local government should build more affordable housing and improve public transport.
d. My hometown has changed a lot over the past ten years.
e. As a result, more and more people from nearby villages have come to live and work here.
A. b – d – e – a – c
B. d – e – b – a – c
C. d – b – a – e – c
D. d – b – e – a – c
Đáp án: D
Giải thích: d – b – e – a – c. d là câu chủ đề; b “First” nêu thay đổi; e “As a result” chỉ kết quả; a “However” chuyển sang mặt tiêu cực; c “In my opinion” nêu ý kiến cuối.

4.
Dear Linh,
a. When you arrive, I will show you around the Old Quarter, which dates back hundreds of years.
b. I’m delighted to hear that you will visit Ha Noi next month.
c. I can’t wait to see you and hope you will have a great time here.
d. In addition, please remember that the city is very crowded during rush hour, so you should avoid travelling then.
e. Since you last visited, the city has changed a lot, and many high-rise buildings have been built.
Best wishes,
Hoa
A. b – e – a – d – c
B. b – a – e – d – c
C. e – b – a – c – d
D. b – e – d – a – c
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: b – e – a – d – c. b vui khi bạn sẽ đến; e “Since you last visited” nói về thay đổi; a “When you arrive” hứa dẫn đi tham quan; d “In addition” thêm lời khuyên; c lời chúc cuối thư.

5.
a. Many local people use it every day to avoid traffic jams.
b. The Ha Noi Metro opened to the public in 2021.
c. For this reason, the city is planning to build more lines by 2030.
d. In particular, it is faster and more reliable than buses.
e. Moreover, it produces less pollution, which is good for city residents.
A. b – d – a – e – c
B. b – a – d – e – c
C. a – b – d – e – c
D. b – a – e – d – c
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: b – a – d – e – c. b giới thiệu tuyến Metro; a “it” nói về người sử dụng; d “In particular” giải thích lý do; e “Moreover” thêm lợi ích; c “For this reason” nêu kế hoạch.

6.
a. Mr Hung: I’m sorry, but there’s nothing we can do about it. The road works will finish next month.
b. Nam: Excuse me, I want to complain about the noise from the building site next door.
c. Nam: I see. I hope it won’t take too long.
A. b – c – a
B. a – b – c
C. b – a – c
D. c – a – b
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: b – a – c. b nêu phàn nàn; a là phản hồi tiêu cực “nothing we can do about it”; c đáp lại.

7.
a. Hoa: Not only that, there are two new parks and a big shopping centre, so people can enjoy more leisure activities.
b. Lan: Wow, the city has really changed since I left three years ago!
c. Lan: That sounds great. Shall we go to the park this weekend?
d. Hoa: Sure! But the more people come here, the more crowded the parks become, so let’s go early.
e. Hoa: I know. A lot of high-rise buildings have been built recently.
A. b – a – e – c – d
B. b – e – a – c – d
C. e – b – a – c – d
D. b – e – a – d – c
Đáp án: B
Giải thích: b – e – a – c – d. b ngạc nhiên về thay đổi; e “I know.” nêu nhà cao tầng; a “Not only that” thêm công viên và trung tâm mua sắm; c đề nghị đi công viên; d đồng ý và gợi ý đi sớm.

8.
a. For example, city residents have more job opportunities and better schools.
b. Urbanisation has brought both advantages and disadvantages to Viet Nam.
c. In conclusion, the government should invest in public transport and affordable housing to solve these problems.
d. On the other hand, the cost of living is going up, and air pollution is becoming more and more serious.
e. First of all, it has helped many people improve their lives.
A. e – b – a – d – c
B. b – a – e – d – c
C. b – e – a – d – c
D. b – e – d – a – c
Đáp án: C
Giải thích: b – e – a – d – c. b là câu chủ đề; e “First of all” nêu lợi ích; a “For example” minh họa; d “On the other hand” nêu mặt tiêu cực; c “In conclusion” kết luận.

9.
Dear Sir or Madam,
a. Buses are always late, especially during rush hour, so many residents cannot get to work on time.
b. I am writing to complain about the bus services in my neighbourhood.
c. I look forward to hearing from you soon.
d. I would be grateful if you could improve the service and add more buses on this route.
e. Many local residents have waited for more than thirty minutes this week.
Yours faithfully,
Nguyen Van An
A. b – e – a – d – c
B. a – b – e – d – c
C. b – a – d – e – c
D. b – a – e – d – c
Đáp án: D
Giải thích: b – a – e – d – c. b nêu mục đích viết thư; a nêu vấn đề; e dẫn chứng (this week); d đề nghị cải thiện; c “I look forward to hearing from you” kết thư.

10.
a. Since then, the urbanisation rate has continued to increase gradually.
b. Urbanisation in Malaysia started in the early 1970s.
c. After a period of rapid growth, the urban and rural populations were almost equal in the early 1990s.
d. At that time, only about 26 per cent of the population lived in urban areas.
e. Today, more than 77 per cent of Malaysia’s population live in urban areas.
A. b – d – c – a – e
B. b – c – d – a – e
C. d – b – c – a – e
D. b – d – a – c – e
Đáp án: A
Giải thích: b – d – c – a – e. b mở đầu (đầu thập niên 1970); d “At that time” nêu 26%; c giai đoạn tăng nhanh; a “Since then” tăng dần; e “Today” số liệu hiện tại.
